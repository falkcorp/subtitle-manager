### Changed

#### Go 1.27

Org standards prefer Go 1.27 wherever every third-party library and tool
supports it, and this repo's dependency graph does. `go.mod` and every CI
`go-version` pin move together.

1.27 is also where `encoding/json/v2` stops being a `GOEXPERIMENT` and becomes
part of the standard library.

Verified before bumping rather than after: `go build ./...`, `go vet ./...` and
the full `go test ./...` all pass under Go 1.27.
